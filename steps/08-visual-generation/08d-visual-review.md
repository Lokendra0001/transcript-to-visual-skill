# 08d — AI Image Review and Refinement

Inspect the actual generated PNG side by side with the selected template at comparable size, plus crops of dense sections. For T01 also compare the approved concept as a visual quality benchmark. Save a comparison image and identify all inspected files in `visual-review.json`.

Assess composition, proportions, hierarchy, panel placement, color, typography, readable text, connectors, logos and outcome emphasis. Compare every required item and relationship to the inventory and every logo to its prepared Step 07 source; identify omissions, spelling errors, invented claims, incorrect grouping, misleading arrows and altered or unreadable marks. Record evidence and a pass or fail for each check. Do not equate a plausible image with verified content.

Inspect each tool occurrence against its prepared asset and mapping: record whether the logo is visible, recognizable, correctly associated, prominent relative to its supporting label, and faithful in shape and color. Mark a missing, invented, wrong or unrecognizable logo as failed or unverified, and record each authorized fallback. Inspect outer and nested box borders at native and intended display sizes for continuous edges, sufficient contrast, consistent weight and clean corners; faint, broken or invisible boundaries fail the border check. Save representative logo-tile and border crops linked to the final image fingerprint. A text-only tile where a suitable verified logo exists does not pass the logo-priority requirement.

Compare every repeated brand occurrence to the mapped original prepared file and to other occurrences. A redrawn image, distorted mark, wrong company logo or changed version is not an exact verified source asset; record as failed or unverified. Compare the final image side by side with the user's approved layout and fail material unintended changes to its arrangement, title, panel styling or footer. Keep approved content and the explicit prohibition on CRM placeholder bars in force even when the example image contains bars.

Check logo labels in the final image: they should be small, normal-weight and readable, not bold, oversized or visually dominant over their logos. If labels are too small to read or appear bold and dominant, refine and recheck the affected tiles.

Review the full image and dense crops at the intended display size. Specifically fail cramped text, indistinct fonts, collisions, clipped or illegible labels, skeleton bars or other placeholder content, unapproved CRM details, and a missing, cropped, or inaccurate required footer. For this ABM run, explicitly inspect the signal panel, CRM panel and bottom footer; gray bars in the CRM panel are an automatic failure. Record each crop's source image SHA-256, region, actual inspection result and exact defect. Check the actual bottom crop against T01, including its portrait, name, badge and company mark. Inspect empty or sparse panels for intentional sizing and visual balance. A taller canvas is acceptable; document that adaptation instead of penalizing it as a fidelity failure. Refine these problems automatically within the bounded cycles before asking for input; request wording only when genuinely needed to resolve a content ambiguity, not merely to fill space.

The review and validation report must refer to the SHA-256 and dimensions of the exact final PNG. An older review, stale crop, or report whose fingerprint does not match the current image is invalid. A `pass` for readability, copy or footer requires actual inspection evidence; if that evidence is missing, set the check to `not_reviewed` and the overall status to incomplete. Do not promote an image to successful merely because an edit was attempted or a footer was added.

Record planned versus actual pixel dimensions, intended viewing size and observed typography in the final review. Inspect the smallest labels at native resolution and at the intended display size for blur, distorted or misspelled glyphs, low contrast, overlap and clipping; inspect the whole image for balanced spacing. Do not claim a particular font family or font size was achieved unless independently measurable. If a readable single image cannot be generated within the tool's actual limits, mark the output incomplete and explain the limitation; ask about an alternative format only after supported refinement options have been exhausted.

Use targeted AI image edits or regeneration for defects, with at most three bounded refinement cycles. Inspect the actual final image and record the attempts, changes and remaining defects. If fidelity, semantics, readable content or asset identity remain materially wrong, mark Step 08 incomplete and present the image only as a draft, explaining the defects. A visually appealing image alone is not a pass.
